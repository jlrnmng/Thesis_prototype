## Getting Started

### What is Hirely?

Hirely is an AI-powered recruitment platform that matches your skills and experience with the perfect job opportunities. Our intelligent matching system analyzes your resume and recommends jobs that best fit your profile.

### Key Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Matching** - Advanced algorithms find the best jobs for you
- 📊 **Match Scores** - See how well you match each job posting
- 📄 **Resume Analysis** - Automatic extraction of your skills and experience
- 🎯 **Personalized Recommendations** - Jobs ranked by relevance to your profile
- ⚡ **One-Click Applications** - Apply to multiple jobs quickly

## Creating Your Account

## Uploading Your Resume

### Why Your Resume Matters

Your resume is the foundation of Hirely's matching algorithm. The better your resume, the more accurate your job recommendations will be.

### Resume Best Practices

#### Format Guidelines
- **Use PDF format** - Ensures compatibility
- **Clear structure** - Use headings and bullet points
- **Standard fonts** - Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman
- **Adequate spacing** - Easy to read, not cluttered

#### Content Guidelines

**✅ DO Include:**
- Your full name and contact information
- Professional summary or objective
- Work experience with dates and descriptions
- Educational qualifications
- Technical and soft skills
- Relevant certifications
- Projects (especially for IT/tech roles)

**❌ DON'T Include:**
- Photos (unless required in your region)
- Overly decorative elements
- Tables (may not parse correctly)
- Scanned images of text
- Personal information like SSN or ID numbers

#### Technical Skills Section

The AI analyzes technical terms, so be specific:
- Instead of "programming," write "Python, JavaScript, React"
- Instead of "databases," write "MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB"
- Use industry-standard terminology

### Updating Your Resume

You can update your resume anytime:

1. Go to your **Profile** page
2. Scroll to **"Resume & Documents"** section
3. Click **"Choose File"** to select new resume
4. Click **"Update Profile"**
5. The system will re-analyze your resume and update your match scores

## Understanding Match Scores

### What is a Match Score?

A match score is a percentage (0-100%) that indicates how well your profile aligns with a job posting.

### How Scores Are Calculated

Hirely uses a hybrid AI algorithm combining:

1. **Semantic Similarity (70%)** 
   - Analyzes the meaning and context of your resume
   - Compares your experience with job requirements
   - Understands similar but different terms (e.g., "developer" and "engineer")

2. **Keyword Matching (30%)**
   - Looks for specific technical skills
   - Matches exact terms and phrases
   - Weights important keywords higher

### Score Ranges

- **80-100%** - Excellent Match 🟢
  - You meet most or all requirements
  - Highly recommended to apply

- **60-79%** - Good Match 🟡
  - You meet many requirements
  - Consider applying if interested

- **40-59%** - Moderate Match 🟠
  - Some alignment with requirements
  - Apply if you're willing to learn

- **Below 40%** - Low Match 🔴
  - Significant skill gaps
  - Not recommended unless you're confident

### Match Explanation

Click any job to see:

**Matched Skills:**
- Programming languages (Python, JavaScript, Java)
- Tools and technologies (React, Docker, AWS)
- Soft skills (leadership, communication)

**Matched Experience:**
- Industry keywords
- Job functions
- Years of experience indicators

**Matched Education:**
- Degree requirements
- Certifications
- Educational institutions

## Applying for Jobs

### How to Apply

1. **Browse jobs** on your dashboard
2. **Click a job** you're interested in
3. **Review the full details** and match explanation
4. **Click "Apply Now"** button
5. **Confirm your application**

### What Happens After You Apply

1. **Application Submitted**
   - Your resume is sent to the employer
   - Application status is tracked

2. **Employer Reviews**
   - Admin/employer sees your profile
   - They can view your resume
   - They review your match score

3. **Shortlisting**
   - Employers can shortlist top candidates
   - You may be contacted for interviews

### Checking Application Status

Currently, you can:
- See which jobs you've applied to
- Track application submissions

**Note:** Interview scheduling and further communication happens via email.

### Application Tips

✅ **Before Applying:**
- Read the full job description
- Check the match explanation
- Ensure you meet minimum requirements
- Update your resume if needed

✅ **After Applying:**
- Keep your email inbox active
- Respond promptly to employer contacts
- Keep your profile updated

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

**Problem:** "Invalid email or password" error

**Solutions:**
- Double-check your email for typos
- Ensure Caps Lock is off for password
- Try resetting your password (if available)
- Contact support if account was recently created

---

**Problem:** Cannot access dashboard after login

**Solutions:**
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Check if you're logged in as admin instead of user
- Logout and login again

---

### Resume Upload Issues

**Problem:** Resume upload fails

**Solutions:**
- Ensure file is PDF format
- Check file size (must be under 10 MB)
- Remove any password protection from PDF
- Try converting to PDF using a different tool
- Ensure filename has no special characters

---

**Problem:** Match scores are very low

**Solutions:**
- Update your resume with more details
- Add specific technical skills
- Include relevant keywords from job descriptions
- Ensure resume is well-formatted and readable
- List all relevant experience and education

---

### Application Issues

**Problem:** Cannot apply to a job

**Solutions:**
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check if you've already applied
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache

---

### Performance Issues

**Problem:** Page loads slowly

**Solutions:**
- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Close unnecessary browser tabs
- Try accessing during off-peak hours
- Disable browser extensions temporarily

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is Hirely free to use?**  
A: Yes, creating an account and applying for jobs is completely free for job seekers.

**Q: How many jobs can I apply to?**  
A: There's no limit! Apply to as many jobs as you're interested in.

**Q: Can I update my resume after registering?**

**Q: How often are new jobs posted?**

### Privacy & Security

**Q: Is my personal information secure?**  
A: Yes, Hirely uses industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

**Q: Who can see my resume?**  
A: Only employers whose jobs you've applied to can view your resume.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**  
A: Contact system administrator for account deletion requests.

**Q: Will my data be shared with third parties?**  
A: No, your information is only shared with employers you apply to.

### Matching Algorithm

**Q: How accurate is the match score?**  
A: The AI-powered algorithm is highly accurate, but use your judgment too. A 60% match might still be a great opportunity!

**Q: Why don't I see any high match scores?**  
A: This could mean:
- Limited jobs matching your profile
- Resume needs more detail
- You're in a specialized field with fewer postings

**Q: Can I improve my match scores?**  
A: Yes! Update your resume with:
- More specific skills
- Detailed work experience
- Relevant keywords
- Current certifications

**Q: What if I don't have the exact skills listed?**  
A: Apply anyway if you're willing to learn! Many employers value potential and enthusiasm.
